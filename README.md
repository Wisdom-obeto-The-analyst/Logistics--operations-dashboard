# 🚛 Logistics Operations Dashboard — Power BI

## Project Overview
A professional 4-page logistics operations dashboard built in 
Microsoft Power BI, analysing 400,000+ records across 14 
relational tables covering fleet, driver, safety, fuel and 
customer operations.

## Dashboard Pages
| Page | Focus |
|---|---|
| Executive Summary | Revenue, Profit, Trips, Customers |
| Driver & Safety Performance | On-Time Rate, Incidents |
| Fleet & Fuel Efficiency | MPG, Maintenance, Utilization |
| Customer & Route Analysis | Top Customers, Route Revenue |

## Key Metrics
- 💰 Total Revenue: $298.62M
- 📈 Total Profit: $197.30M
- 🚛 Total Trips: 85,000
- 👥 Total Customers: 200
- ⛽ Avg MPG: 6.50
- ✅ Fleet Utilization: 76.67%

## Key Findings
- Business operates at a strong 66% profit margin
- On-time delivery rate of 55.67% is significantly below 
  the 95% industry benchmark
- Fuel costs of $95.59M represent 94% of total costs — 
  efficiency improvements could save millions
- First Group is the top customer at $10.4M revenue
- RTE00044 is the highest performing route at $11.2M

## Tools & Skills Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX Measures & Calculated Columns
- Power Query Data Cleaning
- Relational Data Modelling (14 tables)
- Business Insight Generation
